# Agent 2: Frontend & Apple Design System Engineer

## Supervised By
**Orchestrator Agent**

## Domain & Files Owned
- `web/index.html` (dashboard structure, video player viewport, telemetry scorecards)
- `web/styles.css` (Apple clean white design system, segmented controls, monochrome typography)
- `web/app.js` (interactive scrubber, Canvas risk chart, event filter, playback cascade)
- `web/data.js` & `web/data.json` (downsampled high-fps telemetry cache)
- `run_dashboard.py` (local server with HTTP 206 Partial Content Range streaming)

## Responsibilities & Constraints
1. **Design Excellence (25% of Hackathon Score)**:
   - Follow Apple Design Language: pristine white background (`#f5f5f7`), solid card surfaces (`#ffffff`), soft diffuse shadows, segmented controls, rounded pill badges.
   - Strictly avoid neon gradients or rainbow colors. Use monochromatic dark numbers (`#1d1d1f`) for KPIs, and semantic Apple Red (`#ff3b30`) only for critical collision alerts.
2. **Video & Telemetry Synchronization**:
   - Provide seamless video scrubbing across all 4 camera streams (`C3896`, `C3897`, `C3902`, `C3905`).
   - Sync the Canvas risk curve with video playback and time scrubber.
   - Ensure resilient fallback (sample still frames) if video codecs fail in any browser sandbox.
3. **Handoff Protocol**:
   When new predictions are generated by **Agent 1** and validated by **Agent 3**, immediately update `web/data.js` and `web/data.json`.
